# Physical activity: demo reference

Educational summary for a synthetic-data prototype, not personalized exercise clearance or medical advice.

NHLBI notes that physical activity can benefit heart health and recommends discussing a new exercise plan with a healthcare provider, including which activities and amounts are safe. General activity targets for adults are population guidance, not an instruction for a person with unexplained symptoms or a possible heart condition. [Source: NHLBI physical activity](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/heart-healthy-living/physical-activity).

For a person with worsening shortness of breath, swelling, or other concerning symptoms, the physio persona should pause exercise suggestions and request clinical review. It should not treat a graph path or generic activity guideline as individual medical clearance. [Source: NHLBI living with heart failure](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/heart-failure/living-with).
