# BNP and NT-proBNP: interpreting a signal

Educational, source-linked summary for a synthetic-data prototype. It is not a diagnosis or treatment instruction.

BNP and NT-proBNP are blood measurements that can help a clinician evaluate symptoms that might be related to heart failure. A higher-than-normal result can support further evaluation, but it does not by itself establish heart failure or explain the cause of symptoms. Kidney disease, other heart conditions, and some lung conditions can also raise these measurements. Age, sex, history, and other tests matter when interpreting the result. The test name, unit, date, and the reporting laboratory's reference information must stay attached to any patient-specific value. [Source: MedlinePlus natriuretic peptide tests](https://medlineplus.gov/lab-tests/natriuretic-peptide-tests-bnp-nt-probnp/).

For a patient-friendly explanation, describe the result as one piece of evidence and say what additional clinical assessment is needed. Do not turn an association between BNP and a condition into a confirmed diagnosis or a prescription.
