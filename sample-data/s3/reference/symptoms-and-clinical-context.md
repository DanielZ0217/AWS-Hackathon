# Symptoms are context, not a diagnosis

Educational, source-linked summary for a synthetic-data prototype. It is not triage or medical advice.

NHLBI describes shortness of breath with ordinary activity, difficulty breathing while lying flat, fatigue, swelling of the ankles or legs, and weight gain among symptoms that can occur with heart failure. The pattern and severity differ among people; these symptoms are not specific enough for an agent to name a condition from them alone. [Source: NHLBI heart-failure symptoms](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/heart-failure/symptoms).

New or worsening breathlessness, swelling, or weight changes can warrant clinical reassessment. A patient explanation should state what was reported, what has changed, and what remains unknown. It should direct the person to a qualified clinician for assessment rather than turning a graph connection into a patient-specific diagnosis. [Source: NHLBI living with heart failure](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/heart-failure/living-with).
