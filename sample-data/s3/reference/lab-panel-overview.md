# Blood chemistry and potassium: demo reference

Educational summary for a synthetic-data prototype, not a patient record or medical advice.

A basic metabolic panel (BMP) measures glucose, calcium, sodium, potassium, carbon dioxide, chloride, blood urea nitrogen, and creatinine. Together these measurements can inform assessment of fluid and electrolyte balance, metabolism, and kidney function. An abnormal value can have several explanations; the laboratory's units and reference interval, symptoms, history, and other results are needed to interpret it. [Source: MedlinePlus BMP](https://medlineplus.gov/lab-tests/basic-metabolic-panel-bmp/).

A potassium blood test measures potassium in blood. Potassium supports the function of nerves, muscles, and the heart; the kidneys help regulate its blood level. A high or low result may need clinical review, but one value does not identify its cause. Preserve the original measurement, unit, date, and laboratory range before explaining it. [Source: MedlinePlus potassium blood test](https://medlineplus.gov/lab-tests/potassium-blood-test/).

An agent must never infer a diet change, medication, or diagnosis from a single BMP or potassium result. Escalate any reported critical value for human confirmation.
