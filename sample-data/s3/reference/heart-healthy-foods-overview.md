# Food guidance: demo reference

Educational summary for a synthetic-data prototype, not a personalized diet plan or medical advice.

NHLBI describes a heart-healthy pattern built around vegetables, fruit, whole grains, and other nutrient-rich foods while limiting sodium, saturated fat, and added sugars. The appropriate amounts depend on the individual and any conditions or clinician-imposed restrictions. [Source: NHLBI heart-healthy foods](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/heart-healthy-living/healthy-foods).

For someone already diagnosed with heart failure, a clinician may advise limits on salt or fluids. An agent must not invent these restrictions or recommend a specific potassium intake from a lab result alone; it should refer such decisions to the care team. [Source: NHLBI living with heart failure](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/heart-failure/living-with).
