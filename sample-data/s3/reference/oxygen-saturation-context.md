# Oxygen saturation and measurement context

Educational, source-linked summary for a synthetic-data prototype; not a threshold or treatment protocol.

Pulse oximetry estimates blood oxygen saturation with a sensor, often placed on a finger. It helps identify when oxygen may be too low, but a reading should be interpreted with the person's symptoms and clinical circumstances. A reported low value or concerning breathing symptoms should be checked and escalated for qualified human assessment; an agent must not silently normalize it or use this reference to prescribe oxygen. [Source: MedlinePlus pulse oximetry](https://medlineplus.gov/lab-tests/pulse-oximetry/).

Store the value, unit or percent sign, time, device/source, and any reported measurement limitations with the patient artifact. This document contains no patient measurement and does not define a universal red-flag cutoff.
